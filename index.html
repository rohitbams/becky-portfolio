Becky Johnson

Becky Johnson (she/they) is a community driven artist and producer who works within the film, arts and music sector. Becky's work seeks to increase exposure, access and connectivity between different communities and the arts. Their work explores portraiture and encounters, through documentation of people, places and experiences; most commonly in collaboration with other individuals or community groups. Becky's work often centres around documenting dance, especially in social settings. They seek to archive the connections on the dancefloor alongside the communities which host them.
